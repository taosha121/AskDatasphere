Lines of Business (LoB)

The industry and cross-application packages offer selective access to line-of-business information already to the degree that this is required for the scenarios to work. Here in particular finance information from SAP S/4HANA Cloud and HR information from SuccessFactors are available.

The line-of-business connections, e.g. to SAP S/4HANA Cloud or SAP SuccessFactors, and the data models on top may be re-used even without an industry or cross-application focus.
