Data Sharing Cockpit - Data Provider's Guide

Users with a modeler role can create a data provider profile and publish data products to the Catalog & Marketplace.

Note

The Catalog & Marketplace, and the Data Sharing Cockpit are only available in SAP Datasphere tenants provisioned since January 25, 2021. See 3148585 for more information.

Overview

The Data Sharing Cockpit is the tool you use as the data provider. Together with the consumer-centric Catalog & Marketplace, it is fully integrated into SAP Datasphere. As a data provider, your main objective is to offer valuable data products to the users of Catalog & Marketplace in SAP Datasphere.

See the blog Get yourself up to speed with the SAP Datasphere data marketplace to get more information about SAP Datasphere Data Products.

What you will find in here

Before you can start selling data products, you need to create an informative data provider profile. It's your digital business card that consumers can use to get in contact with you, for example to request activation keys for licenses or ask product-related questions.

To fill your digital shelves with data products, you have to create them in a form-based UI. In the creation process you decide whether your data products are either for free or require the purchase of a license at a certain cost. It's your decision to ship your data products as one-time deliveries or as regularly updated releases.

Being a data provider includes recurring administrative tasks like managing the lifecycle of your data products and processing requests for new licenses and activation keys by users who want to purchase and download your data products.

For more information, see The Data Sharing Cockpit.
